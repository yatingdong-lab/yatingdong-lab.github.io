School of Life Sciences · Zhejiang Chinese Medical University

We study how plants adapt to their environment, and how that knowledge can shape the next generation of medicinal crops.

Plants have evolved a remarkable molecular vocabulary to sense, respond to, and ultimately thrive in changing environments. Our group reads this vocabulary using high-throughput omics, phenomics, and systems biology. We work across systems ranging from classical models such as cotton and Marchantia to medicinal plants, where understanding adaptation translates directly into better breeding and more sustainable cultivation.
